Lime Jello Fudge

A tasty treat from yesteryear!

Curated by Andrew McIlroy

Baked and featured by B. Dylan Hollis

Prep:   10 Mins    Cook:   15 Mins    Chill:   2 Hrs    Total:   2 Hrs 25 Mins



Ingredients
3.5 Cups Granulated Sugar
1/4 Tsp Baking Soda
3 oz Lime Jello Powder
1.5 Cups Milk
.5 Cup Butter


Directions

  1. Place sugar, baking soda, Jello and milk in a large saucepan. Use one larger than 4 quarts if you have it. 3½ cups granulated sugar,¼ teaspoon baking soda,3 ounces lime Jell-o powder,1½ cups milk

  2. Cook the Jello mixture over medium heat until it reaches 234℉. Stir frequently to keep it from sticking to the bottom and scorching. Be very careful as the mixture is hot and will get foamy.

  3. Once the mixture reaches 234℉, remove it from the heat. Pour into a bowl and add the butter. Beat until the mixture thickens a bit.

  4. Pour into a greased and lined 8-inch square pan. Chill until firm, at least a couple of hours.

  5. Cut into squares. Store your fudge in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 2 weeks or freeze for longer storage.



Tips:

  • Fudge is best stored in a cool, dry place. Refrigerating the fudge can dry it out and make it crumbly.

  • Have fun trying different flavors of Jell-o with this recipe. Orange or cherry fudge would be tasty as well.
